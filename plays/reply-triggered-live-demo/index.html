  • Email 2 is a threaded nudge, one line restating the demo. Show drafts before upload.

4Launch one campaign per vertical

ToolsEmailBisonInstantly (option)Attio
  • Apply core: send rules. One campaign per vertical, split evenly between the two variants, from its own inbox set.
  • Send inside the calling window from the inputs; a yes at 11 pm must not fire a call at 11:05.
  • Write every contact to the CRM with play name, vertical, variant and date.

5The yes fires the product

Toolsn8nClaudeLeadMagicProspeoBlitzAPIProduct APISlack
  • Catch the positive reply with a webhook from the sending tool, or a polling pass every two minutes. Classify it with the Reply Routing play's classifier and apply core: CRM and alerts. Only interested fires.
  • If the demo is a call and the row has no number, read the signature, then run the waterfall. Nothing inside three minutes: a rep asks in the thread and the clock restarts when the number arrives.
  • Call the product API with the one input. Log triggered time, completed time and result URL on the CRM record, and post a Slack card.
  • Text output never goes out without a human review. The reviewer approves or edits in Slack, then the reply goes back in the same thread: three bullets on what the demo showed and a soft ask for 15 minutes.

6Follow up on the demo, not the reply

ToolsPhoneCalendlyAttioSlack
  • If the demo connected (call answered, site opened, report viewed), a rep calls and emails the same day, two or three touches.
  • If not, one retry the next business day inside calling hours, then a dated recall. Never a third automatic attempt.
  • Inbound calls to the product's number count as connected demos even without a reply.

7Measure product touches, not replies

ToolsAttioSlack
  • Weekly: sent, yes replies, demos triggered, demos completed, inbound calls, outputs viewed, meetings, by vertical and by variant. Positive replies are the wrong KPI here; optimizing for them produces a thin stream of lukewarm leads who forget to answer once you send the number.
  • Expect a worse contact-to-lead ratio than a plain offer. Cut verticals where demos trigger but never complete.
  • Log whether each meeting came from the agent, the thread or the website.
  • Apply core: credits and handoff.

Guardrails

  • Never auto-call or auto-build without the yes. A question or an objection goes to a human.
  • Respect calling hours and consent rules in the prospect's region. A yes outside the window fires at the start of the next one.
  • A demo that fails returns an apology from a human within the hour.
  • The product output never carries the Pipeline.tech credit.
  • One campaign per vertical, nothing else on the same domain in the same month.

Example run

User: "/pipeline-reply-triggered-live-demo for an AI receptionist that calls back missed leads; verticals are dental clinics, personal injury law firms and HVAC contractors in Texas and Florida"

  4. Six campaigns live, 2,205 contacts, started Monday.
  5. Week three: 61 yes replies, 58 demos triggered, 49 calls answered. The number variant also produced 34 inbound calls with no reply at all.
  6. Same-day follow-up on 49 connected demos and 34 inbound callers, 27 meetings booked, 14 of them personal injury law.
  7. No-number variant 1.9% yes rate, number variant 1.2% but 2.3x the meetings once inbound calls were counted. Scaled law firms, cut HVAC.
