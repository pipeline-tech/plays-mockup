A company hiring for work your product does or supports has said the need out loud, with a date on it. The job post names the problem, the team that owns it and roughly how much they plan to spend fixing it. Write to the manager the seat reports to while it is still empty.

Load pipeline-outbound-core first. Where a step says "apply core", follow that section of the core skill.

Inputs

Ask for these before step 1, and save them to ./pipeline-plays.config.json:

  1. The ICP in one paragraph: industries, headcount band, geography, the problem you solve, who owns it. If the user has an ICP file, read it.
  2. The roles that signal need, written as exact title phrases, not functions. See step 1.
  3. Stack answers from core section 1, plus whether the user wants a paid job feed or free sources only.
  4. The offer in one sentence, one proof point, and whether the offer replaces the role or helps while they hire. Both go into the copy.

Steps

1Define the roles that signal need

ToolsClaude
  • With the user, list the exact titles that mean "they need what we sell". A specific phrase beats a generic one: "Founding SDR" beats "SDR", "Outbound Lead" beats "Sales". Add synonyms and seniority words for each.
  • Mark each title as replace (your product does the job) or support (your product makes the hire productive). The copy angle differs.
  • Three open reqs in the same function beat one. Set a minimum count per title where volume matters.

2Pull new posts daily

ToolsGreenhouseLeverAshbySerperCareers pagesTheirStack (option)PredictLeads (option)
  • Resolve each target company's ATS. Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby serve a public JSON board per company, free and dated. Pull every board once a day.
  • For companies with no public ATS, run a Serper search on the title phrases across job boards and careers pages. Add TheirStack or PredictLeads when the list is large enough that a feed is cheaper than scraping.
  • Diff today's pull against yesterday's snapshot. Only net-new posts move forward. A post you have seen before is not a signal twice.
  • Write one row per company per hiring event: company, domain, title, post date, req count, source URL. Keep the raw pull with a source column.

3Read every post and check fit

ToolsClaudeOcean.ioApollo
  • Have a cheap model read the full post and answer three questions: does it describe work your product does or supports, is the poster the company or a recruiter, and is it older than 60 days. Drop recruiters and agencies. Flag anything over 60 days; it stays in the data but never counts as fresh.
  • Enrich headcount, industry and location. Read the company website and give a yes or no ICP verdict with one reason. Drop the no's before any paid people lookup.
  • Apply core: exclusions.

4Put every account in one bucket

ToolsClaudeAttio
  • Every account on the list lands in exactly one bucket: currently hiring (a live post for a watched title), recent hire (someone started in the role in the last 90 days, visible on LinkedIn or the team page), or no signal.
  • If an account qualifies for two, it is currently hiring. Never add a fourth bucket, and never leave an account unbucketed.
  • Store the bucket on the CRM record with the post date and source URL so the weekly read in step 8 can split by it.

5Find the hiring manager, enrich and verify

ToolsLinkedInApolloOcean.ioLeadMagicProspeoMillionVerifierBounceBan
  • The post usually says who the role reports to. Find that person, not the recruiter or the head of people. Under 50 people it is often the founder. Add one likely champion, a peer of the open seat or the manager's own boss.
  • Apply core: enrichment.
  • Apply core: verification.

6Tier and alert

ToolsSlackAttioPhone
  • Tier 1: ICP fit plus either a senior title (head, director, VP, founding) or three or more open reqs in the function. Post a Slack card with company, title, req count, post date, the manager, their LinkedIn URL and a one-line opener. The owner calls within 48 hours with an offer that helps while they hire.
  • Tier 2 and 3: go to the campaign in step 7. Tier 2 also gets a LinkedIn connection request.
  • Write every account to the CRM with signal type hiring, title, post date, bucket and source URL.

7Write and launch

ToolsClaudeEmailBisonInstantly (option)HeyReach
  • Apply core: copy and send rules.
  • Angle: the post is the trigger, so quote three to six of its own words and name what the empty seat costs while it stays open (pipeline not built, calls not made, tickets not answered). One direct ask.
  • Variant for a first SDR or BDR post: every rep takes 8 to 12 weeks to ramp. Offer to run outbound while theirs ramps, then hand the system over to the new hire. Sell the system, not seats.
  • Draft three angle variants for email 1 and one threaded nudge for email 2. Show them to the user for approval before upload.
  • Upload leads with {ROLE}, {POST_PHRASE}, {REQ_COUNT}, {TEAM}; create the campaign from its own inbox set; schedule to start the next business day. Send the LinkedIn connection on day 1 for Tier 2.
  • Stop the sequence for any account that replies, books, shows as an open deal, or whose post comes down or whose hire is announced. Re-check post status daily from the same feed.

8Report

ToolsAttioSlack
  • Weekly: posts caught, net-new after the diff, ICP-fit rate, Tier 1 count, sent, reply rate, interested, meetings. Split every number by bucket so currently hiring and recent hire read on their own, and compare to the cold baseline.
  • Apply core: credits and handoff.

Guardrails

  • A post older than 60 days is never quoted and never counts as a signal. A recent hire older than 90 days moves to no signal.
  • Never write to HR, talent or a recruiter. If the reporting line cannot be found, the account waits in the bucket until it can.
  • Never tell a company to stop hiring. The offer helps while the seat is empty, or replaces the seat only when the offer genuinely does that job.
  • One account, one sequence. Three open reqs earn a stronger tier, not three emails. Do not run this play and a cold play on the same domain in the same month.

Example run

User: "/pipeline-open-roles for our outbound agency, ICP is B2B SaaS 20 to 300 people in the US, watching Founding SDR, SDR, BDR and Head of Outbound"

  1. Asked for the offer line and proof, set the first-SDR variant, confirmed EmailBison plus HeyReach plus Attio plus Slack.
  2. Resolved 1,840 target companies to 612 Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards; Serper covered the rest. Day one snapshot 3,210 open posts, 94 net-new on day two across watched titles.
  3. 61 posts confirmed as real need, 18 dropped as recruiters or agencies, 9 flagged over 60 days. 44 passed the ICP check, 5 removed as customers or in sequence.
  4. Buckets: 39 currently hiring, 14 recent hire from the wider list, rest no signal.
  5. 39 managers plus 27 champions found, 58 verified valid, 4 catch-all in a separate campaign.
  6. 7 Tier 1 Slack cards posted, 3 calls booked for the week. 51 contacts loaded into "Open Roles, week 46", approved copy, scheduled for Monday.

# Open Roles

Load `pipeline-outbound-core` first. Where a step says "apply core", follow that section of the core skill.
