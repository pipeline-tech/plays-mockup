One broad campaign to a whole market tells you nothing when it underperforms, because every variable moved at once. Split the same list into cells where industry, persona and receiving mail provider are each held constant, and every campaign becomes its own read. Cut the cells that fail, move volume into the ones that work, and the TAM becomes a weekly stream of qualified replies.

Load pipeline-outbound-core first. Where a step says "apply core", follow that section of the core skill.

Inputs

Ask for these before step 1, and save them to ./pipeline-plays.config.json:

  1. The qualified TAM: output of pipeline-icp-from-won-deals or pipeline-full-tam-map, or a CSV with email, first name, company domain, industry and job title.
  2. The ICP in one paragraph, naming up to three industries and three buyer personas.
  3. One offer per segment: the thing you will send or make for them, plus one proof point. One offer for everything means fewer cells.
  4. Stack answers from core section 1.

ToolsClaudeAttioEmailBison
  • Apply core: lists.
  • Apply core: exclusions. Also strip anyone your sender has ever emailed unless the brief allows retargets. This play is net-new by default.
  • Cap each company at 10 contacts per wave, most senior first; park the rest.
  • Clean merge fields now: title-case names, strip legal suffixes and taglines from company names. Copy assumes clean fields.

ToolsEmailGuardMX lookup (option)
  • Tag every row with its receiving provider. Google MX goes to Google, Microsoft 365 to Microsoft, everything else (Proofpoint, Mimecast, custom, unknown) to Other.
  • Build the matrix: up to 3 industries x 3 personas x 3 providers. One segment per campaign, never mixed. Name campaigns {Industry} | {Persona} | {Provider}.
  • Cell size 50 to 500. Merge a cell under 50 into its neighbour on industry or persona, never on provider. Split a cell over 500 into waves.
  • The provider split uses identical copy across its three cells. It exists so you can reallocate volume mid-flight.

3Enrich and verify

ToolsLeadMagicProspeoMillionVerifierBounceBan
  • Apply core: enrichment, for rows that passed step 1 only.
  • Apply core: verification. Catch-all rows get their own campaign per cell, suffixed | Catch-all.

4Write one offer per segment

ToolsClaude
  • Apply core: copy and send rules.
  • Per industry x persona pair (up to 9), draft three angle variants for email 1. Angles differ in opener, proof and ask, not in synonyms, and share one variable set.
  • Three lines per email: their situation, value with a proof number, one ask. Open like a technician asking a diagnostic question only someone in their seat would recognise, not with a stat or a promise.
  • The ask on email 1 is a value CTA: permission to send something made for them (a list, a mockup, a short video). Never a call. Any later booking link is a "strategy session", never an "audit".
  • Email 2 is one deliberately simple threaded nudge after 3 days. Its only job is a second look; "are you the right person for this" is fine. Reuse no distinctive keyword from email 1.
  • Get approval on the plain-text angles before anything is spintaxed.

5Spintax and QA

ToolsClaudeQA checklist
  • Spintax the approved source only. Same angle count in and out; reword, never extend. Render neighbouring spintax blocks together and fix any combination that reads broken.
  • Subject block per fresh send follows core: copy and send rules.
  • Banned on every spintax path: free, guaranteed, profits, offer, quote, earn, #1. Use "on us" or "put in writing" instead.
  • QA gate before upload: plain text on, open tracking off, 2 steps, body 25 to 55 words excluding sign-off, no greeting prefix, no em dashes, variables populated on every lead, inboxes connected with warmup score 90 or higher. Zero blockers or no launch.

6Upload and launch

ToolsEmailBisonInstantly (option)Attio
  • Create the segment variables first, then upload leads with per-segment values ({SEGMENT_SITUATION}, {SEGMENT_HOOK}). In the EmailBison API, custom variables are posted as {"name": "X", "value": "Y"} and the first step waits 1 day minimum.
  • One campaign per cell, same copy across the three provider cells, its own inbox set. Attach senders (paginate the list), apply the schedule, add the two-step sequence, activate.
  • Apply core: CRM and alerts, with the cell name on every contact.
  • Preview three rendered emails per cell with real lead data first.

7Read results weekly and move volume

ToolsEmailBisonAttioSlack
  • Per cell, log sent, bounce, reply rate, interested, meetings, and emails per interested lead (E2L). Under 1:300 excellent, about 1:500 workable, over 1:1,000 weak. Optimise E2L, not reply volume.
  • Diagnostic chain, in order: bounce above 3% indicts verification (pause, re-verify). Low reply rate indicts the list. Low interested rate with normal replies indicts segment and offer. Interested leads that never become meetings indict the handover.
  • Compare providers. Microsoft often produces near-zero qualified interest. Move its daily volume to the Google and Other cells rather than rewriting.
  • Phase 1 settles the offer across the three angles. Phase 2 holds the winner and tests wording. Phase 3 scales. Success is 2% positive reply or 20% of replies positive. A variant that lifts replies but lowers meetings loses.
  • One or two cells usually carry the run. Scale those, pause the rest, refill with the parked contacts from step 1.
  • Apply core: credits and handoff.

Guardrails

  • Never mix two industries, two personas or two providers in one campaign, even to hit a minimum size.
  • No third step. A new angle launches as a new campaign to the untouched part of the cell.
  • Never ask for a call in email 1.
  • A reply is not a result. Read interested rate and meetings before naming a winner.
  • No retargets inside 30 days, and strip replied, bounced and blacklisted rows before every new wave.

Example run

User: "/pipeline-full-tam-cold-email with the TAM CSV from last week, we sell outsourced bookkeeping to clinics, construction firms and agencies"

  1. Loaded 6,340 companies, 5,120 after the ICP check and exclusions, 7,480 contacts after the 10-per-firm cap.
  2. Tagged 3,910 Google, 2,260 Microsoft, 1,310 Other. 27 cells, 4 under 50 merged into neighbouring personas, leaving 23 campaigns plus 5 catch-all campaigns.
  3. 5,860 verified valid, 410 catch-all kept.
  4. Nine offers approved, 27 angles and 9 nudges spintaxed, 11 broken combinations fixed, QA gate clean on all 28 campaigns.
  5. Launched Monday on 36 inboxes. Week 3: 9,800 sent, 0.9% bounce, 1.8% reply, 22 interested, E2L 1:445. Google 1:290, Microsoft 1:1,630. Microsoft volume moved to Google and Other; clinics x practice manager carried 11 of 22 interested and moved to Phase 2.
