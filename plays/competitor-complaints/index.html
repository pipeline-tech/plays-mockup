Find the companies running that competitor, match each to the complaint its segment keeps raising, and pitch the fix with a concrete reason to leave. Where no tool is detectable, a model infers the likely competitor from the website and the email names it as a question, which earns a reply even when the guess misses.

Never a feature list; feature-only displacement copy produced 0 positives from about 4,400 sends.

  • A theme must be something you fix, with proof. No proof point, no theme.
  • A switch offer is required. No concrete reason to leave in one sentence, no play.
  • The competitor's name never appears in a subject line, and in the body only in the poke variant, as a question.
  • Never say you read their reviews or scanned their site. A poke guess is never written as fact.
  • Do not run this play and Stack Switch on the same domain in the same month.

# Competitor Complaints

A competitor's unhappy customers already feel the problem you fix, and their public reviews tell you the exact words they use for it. Find the companies running that competitor, match each to the complaint its segment keeps raising, and pitch the fix with a concrete reason to leave. Where no tool is detectable, a model infers the likely competitor from the website and the email names it as a question, which earns a reply even when the guess misses.

Load `pipeline-outbound-core` first. Where a step says "apply core", follow that section of the core skill.

## Inputs

Ask for these before step 1, and save them to `./pipeline-plays.config.json`:

3. Stack answers from core section 1, plus whether the user has G2 Buyer Intent.
