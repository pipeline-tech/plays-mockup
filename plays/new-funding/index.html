# New Funding

Fresh money is a budget window that closes. A Form D is due within 15 days of the first sale, so it often lands before the press release. Hiring since the round shows what the money is for, and that names the buyer. One short note inside 48 hours, then wait.

Load `pipeline-outbound-core` first. Where a step says "apply core", follow that section of the core skill.

## Inputs

Ask for these before step 1, and save them to `./pipeline-plays.config.json`:

1. The ICP in one paragraph: industries, headcount band, geography, the problem you solve, who owns it. If the user has an ICP file, read it.
2. Which rounds count. Default: Series A to D. Seed is skipped (rarely budget for a paid GTM stack).
3. Stack answers from core section 1.
4. The offer in one sentence and one proof point. Both go into the copy.

## Steps

### 1. Catch the raise early
Tools: SEC EDGAR, Serper, PredictLeads (optional), Crunchbase (optional)
Flow:
- Form D filings | Pulled daily from EDGAR full-text search; due within 15 days of first sale, so it often lands before the press | with: SEC EDGAR | chips: amount sold, issuer, state, date | tag: free source
- Announced rounds | News search for raises in the trailing 7 days; anything older has been worked by everyone | with: Serper, PredictLeads, Crunchbase | tag: confirm
= One row per company | Domain resolved in its own pass, since asking inline costs recall | chips: name, domain, amount, round, lead investors, source URL

- Pull new Form D filings daily from EDGAR full-text search (`https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q=...&forms=D`, free, no key). Keep offerings with amount sold above the user's floor (default 2M USD) and an issuer in the ICP geography.
- Confirm or add rounds with a Serper news search for "raises" OR "funding" OR "Series" in the trailing 7 days.
- Older than 7 days: skip. It has been worked by everyone.
- Write one row per company: name, domain (resolve it in a separate pass, asking inline costs recall), amount, round, lead investors, filing or article URL, date.

### 2. Filter to your ICP

- Apply core: exclusions.

### 3. Read where the money goes

- Roles opened since the round | The teams hiring are what the money funds | with: Serper, Careers pages, LinkedIn | chips: function, seniority, post date
